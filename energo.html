01 / Overview

What we built and why

The EV market is growing fast, but charging infrastructure remains fragmented. Owners spend up to 40 minutes searching for an available station. We built a single app to fix that.

🎯
Problem

EV owners don't know where to find available charging, how long to wait, or how much to pay — the information is scattered across dozens of services.

💡
Solution

A unified map with real-time charging status, smart filtering, reservations, and charge monitoring — all in one app.

📱
My Role

Conducted user research, shaped the product concept, designed all key flows, and supported development through to launch.

02 / Problem

The pain we were solving

"I drove to 5 stations. Two had no power, one had a queue. I just want to charge my car."
— User, CustDev interview
01
No live station status

Apps show outdated data — users arrive to find occupied or broken connectors.

02
Opaque pricing

Rates are hidden until charging begins. The final bill surprises users.

03
Different apps for different networks

Each charging network requires a separate account and payment. 5–7 apps on one phone.

03 / Research

What users say

Mixed-method research: in-depth interviews with EV owners, diary studies, and competitive analysis.

Insight 01
Trust beats speed

78% of users said they'd drive further to a reliable station rather than risk a closer one. Reliability is the key value — not search speed.

Insight 02
Wait time is productive time

65% use charging time productively: coffee, work, walks. The app should notify them — not keep them glued to the screen.

Insight 03
First experience defines everything

If the first charge went smoothly, users return. Onboarding and the first flow are critical for retention.

Insight 04
Community solves the data problem

Users actively share real-time info on Telegram and forums. UGC station reviews are an untapped niche.

Wireframes Usability test Iteration
Phase 04
UI & Design System

Built a design system with 60+ components. Hi-fi Figma prototype with interactive states. Final testing with 12 participants.

Design System Hi-fi Handoff
Decision 01
Real-time availability indicator

Colour system on the map: green = free, yellow = freeing up soon, red = occupied. Reduces wasted trips by 60%.

Decision 02
Smart booking

Reserve a spot up to 30 minutes before arrival. Auto-released if the user doesn't show — fair for everyone.

Decision 03
Transparent pricing

Cost visible before charging begins, broken down by kWh, waiting minutes, and service fee. No surprises on the bill.

07 / Results

What changed after launch

The product went through beta testing with 500 users. Key metrics confirmed the hypotheses formed during research.

4.8★
App Store rating

After the first three months post-launch. Top reviews highlight simplicity and information reliability.

–62%
Failed trips to stations

Users arrived at occupied or broken stations 2.6× less often than with competing apps.

78%
Day-30 retention

High retention thanks to predictable experience and smart charge-completion notifications.

🔬
Real-time status is everything

The highest-rated feature was the real-time indicator. This confirmed the core research insight: trust beats speed.

📲
Push notifications are the key trigger

The "5 minutes remaining" notification boosted NPS by 14 points. Users felt in control without watching the screen.

🗺
The map is the primary entry point

87% of sessions start with the map. The search bar was rarely used — navigation should be visual.

💬
UGC increases data trust

Stations with user reviews received 34% more visits. Community is the best data validator.

08 / Next Steps

What we planned next

Based on data from the first months, three directions emerged for the next iteration.

→ V2
Smart route planning

Navigation integration — the app suggests charging stops on long routes based on current battery level.

→ V2
Subscription & pricing plans

Monetisation via subscription with a fixed price per kWh — ideal for daily drivers.

→ V3
Vehicle integration

API integration with Tesla, BMW, Hyundai — automatic connector type and charge level detection, no manual input.
